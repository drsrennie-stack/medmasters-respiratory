Lab · no PhysioEx this week

Where the reference ranges come from

No Pearson this week. Week 1's lab is about the ruler itself: every lab panel you will ever read has a reference range column, and this week you build one by hand, learn what that column claims and what it stays silent about, and watch what happens to "normal" when the population underneath it moves.
