How this exam is scored

True or false

One point each. One term in every statement is marked. If you say false, write the term that makes the statement true. A correct replacement adds half a point of bonus.

Multiple choice

Each question is worth one point and you have four confidence points to spread across the four options however you want. Your score is the share of those four points that landed on the correct answer.

Matching

One point per pair, when the material supports a matching set.

Brain dump

Four prompts, you pick one. The page scans what you wrote and ticks what it found, then you correct the ticks yourself before the grade is final.
