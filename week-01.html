A quick word before you start. This week is deliberately lighter than the ones that follow, because you are learning two things at once: the physiology, and the system this course runs on. The rhythm you practice this week, the note sheet in two colors, the videos, the lab, the Sunday deadline, is the exact same rhythm all fifteen weeks. Learn it now, while the load is gentle, and every week after this one costs you less.

Plan your hours. Honest estimates for a typical student; yours will differ, and that is fine.

  • Getting set up: Pearson access, the syllabus, the welcome tour. 1.5 to 2 hours.
  • The note sheet, both passes, videos included. 4.5 to 6 hours.
  • Your vision board, the video, and two real replies. About 1.5 hours.
  • The book problems, with the AI log. 1.5 to 2 hours.
  • The standards and ranges lab. 1.5 to 2 hours.

That is about 11 to 13 hours. A normal week in this course runs 16 to 20, so if this week fills your schedule, that is your signal to rework the schedule now, not in Week 4.

This week opens Tuesday, September 8. Everything for it posts by then. You are welcome to read ahead; nothing here is due yet.

The six steps, in order

  1. Prepare

    Read the Week 1 note sheet questions first, preview the text, organize your notes, then answer the best you can from what you know, in your first color. One prompt per competency; the rubric is on the sheet. Note sheet posts when the week opens

  2. Watch and read

    Now work the Week 1 notes and videos, and add what you did not know in a second color. Color one is your baseline; color two is what you need to commit to memory. Open the Week 1 notes →

  3. Discuss

    This week it is your digital vision board: one image of who you are and where you are going, posted with a short video introduction. Post by Friday, 10:00 pm; replies by Sunday, 10:00 pm. Open the vision board →

  4. Practice

    End of chapter problems from Silverthorn, Chapter 1, and run your recall cards in the Mastery OS.

  5. Lab

    Your first lab is the standards and ranges lab: build a reference range by hand and learn what that column on every lab panel claims. No Pearson this week; PhysioEx begins in Week 2. Open the reference range lab →

  6. Prove

    The five minute draw and teach practice round, in the exact midterm format, ungraded. Posts when the week opens
