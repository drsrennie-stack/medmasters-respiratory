Session type

Tutor session. A flexible study exam. You choose the question types, the depth, and how hard the grading is.
Blueprint settings
Blueprint mode locks the format and the scoring: one point per true or false item with half a point of bonus for fixing a false statement, four confidence points spread across the options on every multiple choice question worth one point each, one point per matching pair, and a checklist-scored brain dump.

Question types and depth

Balanced answer key. A fresh randomized A, B, C, D sequence is written into the instructions each time you build. It stops the AI from parking the correct answer on B or C, which is a known habit of these models.

How it grades you

Standard. Fair, short feedback after each answer, with a clear explanation of why the correct answer is right.

Scope and report

How the session runs

  1. 1One question appears. No answer shown. You respond and say why.
  2. 2You are graded right away. Correct, partial, or incorrect, with the reasoning behind it.
  3. 3A hint and one retry if you turned that on. One small clue, then another attempt.
  4. 4The correct answer is revealed with an explanation, then the next question starts.
  5. 5An end report gives your score, your weak topics, and what to do before your next session.
