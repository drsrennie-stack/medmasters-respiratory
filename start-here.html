Before you start

This class costs 16 to 20 hours a week

Not because I want it to. The official floor is about 14 hours a week, what the units are worth over our 15 weeks. Doing well takes 16 to 20, and the A that health programs require can take 20 or more.

Online does not mean less time. It means nobody is scheduling it for you. The hours you would have spent in a lecture hall and a lab room still have to happen. They just happen wherever you decide to put them.
